# Marketing & copy standard (baseline)

Quality bar for marketing and copy artifacts (`artifact_kind: marketing`). QC
enforces this; producers follow it. Shipped BASELINE — grows from QC
self-healing fixes and human feedback. Team/project standards override anything
here.

## Truthful (load-bearing — reject on a miss)
- Every claim about the product or service is accurate and supportable — no
  invented metrics, fabricated testimonials, or unsupported superlatives.

## Effective
- One clear audience and a single primary message; a concrete call to action.
- Benefit-led, not just a feature list; opens with the hook, not throat-clearing.

## On-voice & clean
- Consistent brand voice and tone, at a reading level appropriate to the audience.
- No typos, broken formatting, or placeholder copy.
