# General deliverable standard (baseline)

Quality bar for any artifact without a more specific domain (`artifact_kind:
text`, and the neutral default). QC enforces this; producers follow it. Shipped
BASELINE — grows from QC self-healing fixes and human feedback. A more specific
domain standard (code, research, marketing, …) or team/project standards
override anything here.

## Complete & on-contract (load-bearing — reject on a miss)
- Delivers exactly what the task asked for, in the requested form and scope.
- No placeholders, stubs, "TODO", "[fill in]", or lorem ipsum where real content
  was asked for.

## Grounded & honest
- Factual claims are accurate and not overstated; don't assert specifics the
  work can't support.
- Mark assumptions and uncertainty plainly rather than papering over them.

## Clear & coherent
- Logical structure; terms defined; the same thing named consistently.
- Free of self-contradiction and of obvious errors a careful reader would catch.
